Arrow Realm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Arrow Realm is a casual puzzle game developed by SergeAx Team. We do not sell your personal data.

Data Collection

The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Game progress, settings, and streaks are stored locally on your device only.

Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

The app may show ads through:

  • Google AdMob — collects device identifiers and usage data
  • Unity Ads — collects device identifiers and gameplay data

You can opt out of personalised ads via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

App Permissions

The app requests:

  • INTERNET — for loading ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — for connectivity checks
  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback

Children's Privacy

Arrow Realm is not directed at children under the age of 13.

Data Sharing

Data is shared only with ad network partners, solely to serve advertisements, and with Google Play Services. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your score is submitted to the weekly leaderboard. Anonymous crash reports may be sent via Firebase Crashlytics to help fix bugs.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach SergeAx Team through the app's Google Play Store listing.
